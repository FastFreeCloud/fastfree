## Shared Brand Rules

- **Typography:** Roboto only. Body 14px, Label 12px, Headline 20px.
- **Shape:** Small radius 2px, medium radius 4px. One corner radius per view.
- **Icons:** MDI v7 only.
- **Imagery:** Clean flat UI illustration, light background, generous whitespace, soft shadows.
- **Avoid:** Stock photos of people shaking hands, glossy 3D renders, cluttered gradients.
- **Logo:** Never recolour, add gradients/shadows/outlines, stretch, or rotate.
- **Voice:** Direct, practical, confident. Short sentences. No hype words, no exclamation marks.
- **Social formats:** Facebook feed 1.91:1 (1200x628), Facebook square 1:1 (1080x1080), Story 9:16 (1080x1920).
